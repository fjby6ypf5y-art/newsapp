Settings

Feeds

Paste any RSS or Atom URL. Most outlets publish one at /rss, /feed, or /rss.xml.

Alert keywords

Comma-separated. Matching headlines are highlighted and pushed to the top. Partial words work — evacuat catches both “evacuate” and “evacuation”.

Behaviour

Notifications only fire while the app is open in the foreground — iOS Safari cannot wake a home-screen web app on its own. Use the Shortcut (see README) for background alerts.

Fetch relay

News sites block direct browser requests (CORS). Each feed is tried directly first, then through these relays in order, one URL-prefix per line. Leave the defaults unless one stops working.
