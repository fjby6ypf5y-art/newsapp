Your feeds

Tap a feed to edit its name, address or category. A green dot means it answered on the last refresh; red shows what went wrong. Feeds are fetched from your phone, so this is the only place the result is actually true — a feed that works on one network can fail on another.

Settings

Behaviour

Reopening the app after this long goes back to the first category instead of wherever you were. Pull down on the list to refresh just the category you're reading; the ↻ button refreshes every category.

Privacy

Fetch relay

News sites block direct browser requests (CORS). Each feed is tried directly first, then through these relays in order, one URL-prefix per line. A relay sees your IP address and which feeds you fetch, so the fewer feeds that need one, the less anybody learns about your reading. Feeds on a relay are re-tested directly every few hours, in case the publisher adds CORS support.

Backup

Keep this link somewhere. A Home Screen app has its own storage on iOS, separate from Safari, and deleting the icon deletes your feeds with it. Opening the setup link offers to restore your feeds and settings, so add that link to the Home Screen after a reinstall and you are back where you were. Mail it to yourself; it is just a long URL.
